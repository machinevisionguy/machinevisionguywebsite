About

Machine Vision Guy is my independent take on industrial machine vision, written to help you make smarter, better-informed decisions about how you inspect, measure, and automate.

Who is Machine Vision Guy?

I am Eric Parker, Director of Sales and an Executive Board member at Vista Solutions, North America's largest machine vision integrator. I have spent almost a decade in the industrial machine vision industry.

That role keeps me on the forefront of bench testing and deploying new vision technologies in real industrial environments, across every major platform, Keyence and Cognex included, with no allegiance to any one of them. That exposure is why I can tell you what actually works, what does not, and how to filter the noise.

What I promise

  • Objective. Independent guidance, not a sales pitch.
  • Honest answers. What works, what does not, and the parts vendors leave out.
  • Real comparisons. When a competitor is the better fit, I will say so.

What I cover

  • Vision strategy and standards
  • Automated inspection (quality assurance, defect detection)
  • AI-based inspection, and when it is the wrong tool
  • Vision-guided robotics
  • Non-contact measurement and gauging
  • Tracking and traceability

Work with me

If you are planning, buying, or troubleshooting a vision application, I can help you cut through the options and make a decision you can defend, an unbiased second opinion from someone who sees these systems succeed and fail in the field. If that is useful, reach out and we can talk it through.
